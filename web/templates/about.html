About {{.SiteName}}

{{.SiteName}} gathers the most notable news on artificial intelligence, science and technology every 6 hours, together with tutorials on programming and on building apps with AI.

How it is built

  1. A Go program collects the RSS feeds and public APIs of the sources listed below.
  2. It removes duplicates and scores each story by freshness, popularity (Hacker News points), how many sources cover it and the weight of its source.
  3. A free-tier AI reads the text, writes a short summary in its own words, assigns a section and tags from a closed vocabulary, and hides anything off-topic.
  4. A static site is generated and published to GitHub Pages by GitHub Actions.

Things to keep in mind

  • Summaries are automatic and may contain errors or omissions. When only an excerpt is available, the page says so.
  • Full articles are never republished: every story links to the original, which is where the content and the visits belong.
  • Images belong to their sources and are loaded from their servers.
